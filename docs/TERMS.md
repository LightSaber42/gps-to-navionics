Remote Anchor Watch renews automatically for the selected billing period unless you cancel through your store account before the store's renewal cutoff. Your store controls billing, renewal, cancellation timing, refunds, grace periods, and price-change consent. Use **Manage Subscription** in the app or the store's subscription settings to cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Recurring value includes the authenticated boat heartbeat, current armed/alarm/distance/battery state, durable alarm replay, reconnection, connection-loss warning, and on-demand plot-history catch-up on a separate Android shore phone. The shore phone is read-only and does not need its own purchase.

When validation is temporarily unavailable, the app may trust a last-known active Remote entitlement for up to seven days. A billing change does not deliberately disarm an already-active safety session; access is reassessed before a later session. This continuity policy is not a guarantee that operating-system or network interruptions will preserve monitoring.
